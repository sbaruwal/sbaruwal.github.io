Support

Need help with Astro Splitter? Email baruwal.sujit@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I bought coins or Remove Ads but don't see them.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap the gear on the main menu, or pause during a level.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app resets it. Purchases can be restored.

Can I stop personalized ads?
Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when the game asks, or turn it off any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized. Buying Remove Ads removes the ads between levels.
