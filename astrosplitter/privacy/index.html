Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Astro Splitter iPhone game ("the app", "we") handles and why. Short version: we don't run our own servers or collect your personal information. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google and its ad partners collect some device information to show those ads.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. We don't receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the app which items you own so they can be unlocked or restored.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may work with other ad networks to fill ads. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect:

InformationUsed for
Device and advertising identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking)Showing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad
Device details (model, iOS version, language, screen size), IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing ads that fit your device and region, preventing fraud
Ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or watched)Rewarding you for watched ads, reporting and measurement
Crash and performance data from the ad SDKKeeping ads working reliably

How Google handles this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Tracking: the app asks your permission before allowing tracking across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you'll still see ads, but they won't use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EU, UK and similar regions: where required, the app shows a consent form before personalized ads, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings → Privacy Choices.

Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels. Optional "watch an ad" rewards stay available if you want them.

Children

Astro Splitter is rated 13+ and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and want information removed from an ad partner, contact us and we'll help you reach them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
